Mycelium · interactive installation

The Whisper
Network

an underground web of feeling一张地下的情绪之网

  1. 1
    Have a seat. 请坐。
    Take a breath. There is no right way to do this.深呼吸一下。这里没有标准答案。
  2. 2
    Type a whisper — or scan the code. 输入一句悄悄话,或扫码。
    Write a few words about how you feel right now, on the screen here or on your own phone. A spore is born from it.在这里的屏幕上,或者用你自己的手机,写几句此刻的感受。它会长成一颗孢子。
    mycelium.yueyao.design
  3. 3
    Watch your spore meet the others. 看你的孢子和别人相遇。
    It drifts into the field, finds kindred feelings, touches, and grows — alongside everyone who sat here before you.它会漂进田野,找到相似的情绪,彼此触碰、一起生长——和之前坐在这里的每一个人一起。
  4. 4
    Notice what shifts inside you. 感受一下内心的变化。
    Lighter? Heavier? Clearer? If you like, leave a few words about it — on the site or on a paper card.轻了?重了?清楚了一点?愿意的话,在网站上或纸卡上留几句。
  5. 5
    Take a small gift with you. 带走一份小礼物。
    A little something to remember your spore by. Thank you for sharing a moment.一点小东西,留作你孢子的纪念。谢谢你分享这一刻。
Moony, the little mushroom, likes gentle pats on the head. 小蘑菇 Moony 喜欢被轻轻摸头。
